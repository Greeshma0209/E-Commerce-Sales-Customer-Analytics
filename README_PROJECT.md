# E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales and customer transaction data to understand sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product performance, payment methods, returns, discounts, and regional trends.

The project uses **Python, SQL, and Power BI** to clean, analyze, visualize, and generate meaningful business insights from the dataset.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze overall e-commerce sales performance
* Identify top-performing product categories and products
* Analyze customer purchasing behavior
* Compare sales across different regions
* Analyze payment methods and customer demographics
* Study product returns and return rates
* Analyze the impact of discounts and quantity on sales
* Build an interactive Power BI dashboard
* Generate useful business insights for decision-making

## 💡 Key Findings

* Electronics is one of the strongest-performing product categories based on sales.
* South region recorded the highest overall sales.
* December 2024 recorded the highest monthly sales in the analyzed data.
* Credit Card was the leading payment method by sales.
* Most customers were repeat customers.
* Fashion had the highest return rate among the categories.
* Most deliveries were completed within approximately 4–5 days.
* Higher order quantities generally resulted in higher average order values.
* Higher discounts were associated with lower average order values in the analyzed data.
